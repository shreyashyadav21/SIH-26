The Challenge

Why Bailadila Mines Need MineSafe

Severe monsoon fog creates critical safety and productivity challenges for India's largest iron ore producer

🌫️

Zero Visibility Conditions

During June–October monsoon, visibility drops to 3–5 meters on hilltop haul roads, making HEMM operation extremely dangerous.

⚠️

Collision & Accident Risk

Heavy dump trucks operating in zero visibility face extreme collision risks, endangering operators and causing equipment damage.

📉

Production Losses

Forced operational halts during fog lead to increased cycle times, reduced fleet productivity, and significant ore evacuation losses.

🚛

Fleet Coordination Failure

Without V2V communication, multiple dump trucks on the same haul road cannot coordinate safely in fog conditions.

🔦

Inadequate Existing Aids

Conventional fog lights, reflectors, and manual radio calls are insufficient for dense fog environments above 1,000m elevation.

📡

No Real-time Intelligence

Control rooms lack real-time situational awareness — no automated fog monitoring, no instant alerts, no digital fleet view.

Our Solution

MineSafe Technology Stack

An intelligent multi-layer safety platform combining AI, IoT, and V2X communication

1

AI Video Fog Clearing & Dehazing

Real-time Dark Channel Prior & computer vision dehazing engine clears heavy fog from video feeds to reveal the clear real view of haul roads, detect obstacles, and classify visibility.

2

Voice Alert Modulation

Automatic voice announcements to all operators based on fog severity — from speed advisories to emergency halt commands, adapting tone and urgency.

3

GPS Fleet Tracking & V2V

Real-time GPS tracking of all dump trucks with V2V proximity alerts when vehicles come within collision-risk distance during fog.

4

Command & Control Dashboard

Centralized real-time monitoring with live fog gauges, fleet status, alerts, charts, and road condition maps for control room operators.

5

Open-Source ML Training

YOLOv8 + EfficientNet trained on RESIDE and FRIDA fog datasets with mine-specific augmentation for accurate Bailadila-condition detection.
